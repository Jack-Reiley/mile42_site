# #107 — Rename the product from Dewey to Vickee across the site

- Source ticket: https://github.com/Jack-Reiley/mile42_site/issues/107
- Pull request: https://github.com/Jack-Reiley/mile42_site/pull/108
- Parent epic: none
- Delivery unit: unit-107-rename-vickee
- Requirement version: 1

## Objective

The product is called Vickee. Every surface a reader or a search engine meets
says so: visible copy, the route, page titles, accessible names, and the names
of the files the site is built from.

## Scope

- Visible copy, headings, eyebrows, button and link labels, table columns, and
  list items across every page that named the product.
- The route. `/meet-dewey` becomes `/meet-vickee`, with a permanent redirect
  from the old path in `site/public/_redirects` and a matching entry in the
  `MOVED` list in `site/src/App.jsx`.
- Page titles in the `PAGES` table.
- Accessible names and the identifiers behind them: the pillar pane id and its
  `aria-controls` reference, the catalog drawer plate, and the librarian
  diagram's region keys.
- File renames, with imports updated: `MeetDewey.jsx`, `MeetDewey.test.jsx`,
  `DeweyPillars.jsx`, `dewey-entry-points.test.jsx`, and
  `dewey-trademark-retired.test.jsx`.
- Component and local identifier renames: `MeetDewey`, `DeweyPillars`, the
  `DEWEY` route constant, and the `dewey` prop on `LibrarianFlow`.
- The illustration: the source master under `design/illustrations`, the `MAP`
  entry and `key` in `site/scripts/illustrations.mjs`, the `manifest.js` entry,
  and the three generated variants, regenerated by
  `npm run illustrations:build` rather than renamed by hand.
- Comments that name the product to describe it.

## Out of scope

- The librarian, catalog, and stacks metaphor, which stays exactly as written.
  "Vickee, the librarian" now reads as an analogy rather than a pun, and that is
  deliberate for this pass.
- `docs/requirements/*.md`. Six carry the old name in a filename or body. They
  are versioned records of what was true when they shipped, the same reasoning
  #103 applied to #60 and #97 applied to `/working`. This document is named the
  same way for the same reason.
- `copy_prototype/`, a fixed reference that never named the product.
- Anything outside this repository: social profiles, the share card image, which
  carries no product name, and external listings.

## Non-functional requirements

- The redirect is `301`, not `302`, so accumulated link equity transfers.
- The redirect precedes the SPA fallback. Netlify takes the first match, and the
  `200` catch-all would otherwise serve the app at the old path and never emit
  the permanent redirect a crawler needs.
- No change to bundle size, request count, or LCP. The hero's bytes are
  unchanged; only its filename moved.
- Heading hierarchy, landmarks, link semantics, and keyboard reachability are
  unchanged. No anchor became a handler.
- No new dependency.

## Deliberate deviations

- **Two files outside `site/` were changed beyond the illustration master.**
  `design/tokens/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` and `design/tokens/verify/check.mjs` each
  named the product once, in prose describing the current site. The ticket's
  scope said `site/`, but its stated intent was that no path or reference in the
  repository keeps the old name outside `docs/requirements/`. Renaming them
  follows that intent; leaving them would have left the design tokens describing
  a product that no longer exists under that name.
- **The redirect is a fourth permitted mention in SCN-010.** The scenario as
  designed named three preserved citations. A `301` cannot move a path without
  naming it, so `_redirects` and the `MOVED` entry necessarily carry
  `/meet-dewey`. They are listed explicitly in the guard rather than matched by
  a loose pattern.
